Hello, I'm Yuwei Zhao, a Ph.D. student at the [School of Computer Science](https://cs.pku.edu.cn/English/Home.htm), [Peking University](https://english.pku.edu.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Yingfei Xiong](https://xiongyingfei.github.io/).

My current research interests include program analysis, program synthesis, and code optimization. I am particularly interested in combining large language models with program-analysis techniques to make code optimization more effective and reliable.
